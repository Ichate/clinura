WHAT THE CAMERA SEES

Small color changes.
Useful estimates.

Remote photoplethysmography looks for subtle color variation across exposed skin. Clinura tracks several facial regions and checks whether the signal is usable.

01

Find facial regions

Face landmarks help Clinura track the forehead and cheek areas while checking pose and visibility.

02

Read changing light

Color samples over time are filtered to look for pulse-related patterns while accounting for motion and noise.

SET UP FOR A GOOD SIGNAL

Give the camera
a steady view.

Small changes to lighting and camera position make a meaningful difference to signal quality.

01Face a soft light

Use even light on your face. Avoid bright backlighting and flickering sources.

02Keep your face in frame

Sit about an arm's length away and make sure your face is large enough in the preview.

03Stay still while measuring

Head movement, talking and exercise can overwhelm the camera pulse signal.

04Allow a settling period

Pulse, HRV and respiration require several seconds to tens of seconds of usable signal.

i
Camera estimates have limitations

Clinura is a research and wellness prototype, not a medical device. Camera-derived readings can be affected by movement, lighting, skin region visibility and hardware. Do not use them to diagnose or make urgent care decisions.

⌁
Optional thermal camera workflows

Thermal mode is designed for compatible radiometric USB thermal cameras. A standard webcam cannot provide true temperature data.
