IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS

Estimates need
the right context.

Clinura is a research and wellness prototype, not a medical device.

01 / SIGNALMovement and lighting matter

Motion, uneven light, camera noise, low face visibility and skin-region obstruction can weaken or bias estimates.

02 / TIMINGReadings take time

Heart rate, HRV and breathing need adequate stable data windows. A displayed value may be provisional or unavailable.

03 / HARDWAREDevices behave differently

Camera drivers, frame rate, exposure, focus and resolution differ by device and operating system.

04 / DECISIONSNot for diagnosis or emergencies

Do not rely on Clinura for diagnosis, treatment, medication changes or urgent decisions. Use validated medical equipment and professional guidance.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Clear answers.
No guesswork.

Does Clinura upload my camera video?+

No. Live camera frames are processed on the computer running Clinura. Optional AI features send summaries of readings and events, not video frames.

Where is my reading history stored?+

Telemetry and exercise records are stored locally in Clinura's data directory. The README describes the files and how to clear them.

Can I use these readings as medical measurements?+

No. Clinura produces camera-derived estimates and is not a validated medical device. It is intended for research and wellness exploration.

Does a better camera guarantee accurate readings?+

No. Image quality helps, but motion, lighting, fit, skin visibility and algorithm limits still affect the signal.
