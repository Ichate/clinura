A SIMPLE START

Get a clearer
camera view.

Clinura works with a webcam or a compatible phone camera exposed to your computer. Place the camera at eye level, use steady front lighting and sit still while the signal settles.

See camera setup tips ↗
01
Connect a camera

Use the built-in camera, a USB webcam, Mac Continuity Camera, or a supported Android webcam mode.

⌖
02
Find a steady position

Keep your face centered, well lit and comfortably still while the signal is acquired.

◎
03
Read with context

Use quality cues and trends alongside the estimates. Numbers need good signal and stillness.

⌁
04
Explore the rest

Review events, wellness check-ins, prescribed movement sessions and research modes.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

Your video stays
on your computer.

Clinura processes camera frames locally. Video is not sent to the cloud. Optional AI assistance uses a summary of readings and events, not the camera feed.
